Customers should verify payment details before completing a transaction.

If a customer is charged twice for the same order, the transaction should be reviewed by the payment support team.

Unauthorized or suspicious transactions should be escalated to the security team.

Customers should never share passwords, OTPs or complete payment card details with support agents.
